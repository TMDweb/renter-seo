Words used on this board

H1: the main heading of a page. Google reads it to learn the topic. One per page.

Schema (JSON-LD): a hidden code block that describes the page to Google: product, price, business name.

Canonical: the one official address of a page. Every copy must point to it.

404: the answer "page not found". 308 or 301: "moved for good". 307: "moved for now".

Soft 404: a missing page that does not answer 404, for example one that sends visitors to the home page.

Sitemap: the list of addresses we ask Google to crawl.

Staging: the test copy of the site, v2-staging.renter.net.

SSL certificate: the file that gives https and the padlock. It has an end date.

Merchant Center: Google's product account for Google Shopping. It reads our product feed.

4 · Product addresses: copies of every product, deleted products send visitors home

NOT DONEOwner: dev teamTicket with code
What is wrong
Any word after the product number opens the page and calls itself official: /ar/product/2545/4000d/ and /ar/product/2545/anything/ are copies of /ar/product/2545/kamyra-kanwn-4000d/. Deleted products send the visitor to the home page (307). 169 of the 1,139 product addresses in the sitemap are deleted products.

Proof
Live checks and Search Console URL Inspection, Sep 27: /ar/product/1662/120/ (a copy) and the deleted /ar/product/2797/ are both "Submitted and indexed".
Fix
A wrong slug moves for good (308) to the official address. The canonical comes from the product data. A missing product answers 404. The sitemap lists live products only.
Done when
The 4 checks on the ticket page give the expected answers.
