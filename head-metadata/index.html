Words used on this board

H1: the main heading of a page. Google reads it to learn the topic. One per page.

Schema (JSON-LD): a hidden code block that describes the page to Google: product, price, business name.

Canonical: the one official address of a page. Every copy must point to it.

404: the answer "page not found". 308 or 301: "moved for good". 307: "moved for now".

Soft 404: a missing page that does not answer 404, for example one that sends visitors to the home page.

Sitemap: the list of addresses we ask Google to crawl.

Staging: the test copy of the site, v2-staging.renter.net.

SSL certificate: the file that gives https and the padlock. It has an end date.

Merchant Center: Google's product account for Google Shopping. It reads our product feed.

4 · Product addresses: copies of every product, deleted products send visitors home

NOT DONEOwner: dev teamTicket with code
What is wrong
Any word after the product number opens the page and calls itself official: /ar/product/2545/4000d/ and /ar/product/2545/anything/ are copies of /ar/product/2545/kamyra-kanwn-4000d/. Deleted products send the visitor to the home page (307). 99 of the 1,188 product addresses in the sitemap are deleted products.
Why it matters
Google splits a product's ranking across its copies; it indexes two addresses for 11 products. Deleted products stay listed in Google, and a click lands on the home page. Google counts these as soft 404s.
Proof, Oct 11
Live checks of the addresses above. URL Inspection: /ar/product/1662/120/ (a copy) and the deleted /ar/product/2797/ are still "Submitted and indexed".
Fix
A wrong slug moves for good (308) to the official address. A missing product answers 404, the way unknown category addresses do since Oct 11. The sitemap lists live products only.
Done when
The 4 checks on the ticket give the expected answers.
