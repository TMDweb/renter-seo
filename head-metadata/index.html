Words used on this board

H1: the main heading of a page. Google reads it to learn the topic. One per page.

Schema (JSON-LD): a hidden code block that describes the page to Google: product, price, business name.

Canonical: the one official address of a page. Every copy must point to it.

404: the answer "page not found". 308 or 301: "moved for good". 307: "moved for now".

Soft 404: a missing page that does not answer 404, for example one that sends visitors to the home page.

Sitemap: the list of addresses we ask Google to crawl.

Staging: the test copy of the site, v2-staging.renter.net.

SSL certificate: the file that gives https and the padlock. It has an end date.

Merchant Center: Google's product account for Google Shopping. It reads our product feed.

15a · Yearly rent is saved as a monthly price

NOT DONEOwner: Jan (data), Paulo (page)
What is wrong
Some property listings store the full yearly rent in the monthly price, with a 12-month term. The page then shows the yearly total as a price per month, and the product schema marks it as monthly (unitCode: MON).
Example
Product 2947 shows 37,000 SAR per month. The API returns {"price": "37000", "duration": "12", "lease_term": "monthly"}, and the page schema says unitCode: MON.
How many
21 property listings have a monthly price of 15,000 SAR or more with this pattern (public listings feed, read Oct 30, 2026). Other examples: 2914 (90,000), 2577 (80,000), 2011 (120,000), 1825 (200,000).

Renters see a monthly price 12 times too high. Google reads the wrong price from the schema. The blog price tables cannot use these listings.
