Duplicate listings: what to keep and what to redirect

SHEET READY, FOR PAULO AND JANChecked live Nov 8, 2026 on all 1,348 products in the Arabic sitemap: 3 groups of exact duplicates, 6 listings, 3 addresses to redirect, from 3 lessors. Saiaf promised this sheet in the Nov 1 meeting (P9), so the copies are not removed at random.

In short

The sheet
renter-duplicate-listings-2026-11-08.xlsx. One row per address to redirect, with the address to keep, the product ids, the lessor id, the title, the match reason and the Search Console clicks and impressions for 90 days.
What counts as a duplicate
All of these must match: same lessor; same title after normalizing spaces, punctuation and letter forms; same lease terms and prices; and the same photos or the same description (identical, or 95% the same text). A lessor who posts the same item twice on purpose, with a different title, price, photos and text, is not flagged. On this check 3 more pairs have the same lessor, title and price but different photos and text (3648 and 3649, 1536 and 1538, 1220 and 1221), so they stay.
Which address to keep
The listing with the most Search Console clicks in the last 90 days (Aug 10 to Nov 7, 2026, Arabic and English addresses added together). If no listing in the group has clicks, the oldest listing. 0 of the 3 groups keep a listing because of its clicks; the others keep the oldest.
The fix
1. 301-redirect each redirect address to its keep address, and the English address of the same product id too. 2. Remove or unpublish the redirected listings, and take them out of the sitemaps and the Merchant feed.
Done when
Every redirect address in the sheet answers 301 to its keep address, and none of them is in the sitemap or the feed. Then tell Saiaf; TMD checks every row live.
