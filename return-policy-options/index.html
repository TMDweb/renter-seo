Return policy: three wordings for Mr. Mohamed to choose from

OPTION A LIVEMr. Mohamed approved option A on Oct 18: the renter returns the item at the end of the rental, the return fee is 0 SAR, and if the item does not match the listing the renter may return it within 1 day for a full refund. Saiaf sent the text to Paulo on Oct 25, and Paulo put it live on Nov 1. Checked Nov 5, 2026: the Arabic and English pages carry it. Next step: Saiaf sets the same values in Google Shopping (Merchant Center ticket).

In short

The problem
Google rejects the return policy until the page shows a number of days, and the Google Shopping settings show the same number. Since Nov 1 the page shows 1 day; the settings must now match it.
The concern
Mr. Mohamed does not want a line that gives every renter extra days or a new right to a refund.
How the options answer it
Each option limits the return to one clear case, so the number of days does not become a general right.
After the choice
Option A was chosen on Oct 18. Paulo puts the sentence on /ar/return-policy/ and /en/return-policy/ (done Nov 1, checked Nov 5). Saiaf then sets the same values in Google Shopping and resubmits. Review takes up to 10 days.
