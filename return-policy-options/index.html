Return policy: three wordings for Mr. Mohamed to choose from

DRAFT READY, MR. MOHAMED DECIDESIn the Oct 11 meeting Mr. Mohamed asked for A, B, C examples to pick from. The three drafts are below. On the agenda of the Oct 18 meeting: Mr. Mohamed picks one, then Saiaf sets the same number of days in Merchant Center. These are drafts for his decision, not legal advice.

In short

The problem
Google rejects the return policy until the page shows a number of days, and the Google Shopping settings show the same number. Today the settings say 1 day and the page says "end of the rental".
The concern
Mr. Mohamed does not want a line that gives every renter extra days or a new right to a refund.
How the options answer it
Each option limits the return to one clear case, so the number of days does not become a general right.
After the choice
Paulo puts the chosen sentence on /ar/return-policy/ and /en/return-policy/. Saiaf sets the same values in Google Shopping and resubmits. Review takes up to 10 days.
