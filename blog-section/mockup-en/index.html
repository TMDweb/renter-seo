Monthly car rental prices in Riyadh 2026: the complete price guide

By Renter TeamPublished Oct 20, 2026Updated Oct 20, 20266 min read

Monthly rental for an economy car in Riyadh starts at SAR 1,800, and a mid-size car starts at SAR 2,500. This guide explains the differences between classes and what changes the price.

Average monthly prices by class

Short answer: an economy car costs SAR 1,800 to 2,400 a month, a mid-size car SAR 2,500 to 3,500, and an SUV from SAR 4,000 (lessor prices on Renter, October 2026).

What changes the price

1. Model and year

The gap between a 2024 and a 2026 model of the same car reaches 15% on a monthly rental.

2. Included mileage

Most lessors include 3,000 km a month. Each extra 1,000 km adds SAR 150 to 300.

Monthly or daily rental

For any period longer than 12 days, a monthly rental costs less in most cases.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a monthly car rental in Riyadh?

From SAR 1,800 for economy cars to more than SAR 4,000 for SUVs, depending on the model and mileage.

Does a monthly rental include insurance?

Most lessors include basic insurance. Comprehensive cover is an optional extra.

What do I need to rent a car monthly?

A valid national ID or iqama, a valid driving licence, and a payment card for the deposit.
