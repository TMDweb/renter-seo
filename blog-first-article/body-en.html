If you need a car in Riyadh for more than two weeks, a monthly rental is usually cheaper than renting by the day. This guide shows monthly car rental prices as lessors list them on Renter, by car class and model. It then explains when a monthly rental makes sense and what to check in the contract before you drive away.

How much is a monthly car rental in Riyadh?

Short answer: a small economy car, such as a Kia Pegas or Hyundai Accent, rents for about SAR 2,000 a month in Riyadh. Prices run from SAR 1,300 to SAR 3,000, depending on the model and year. Mid-size sedans cost SAR 2,500 to 3,240, SUVs SAR 1,900 to 4,900, and large sedans such as the Ford Taurus SAR 5,600 to 6,500.

These figures come from 33 live passenger-car listings in Riyadh on Renter, all with a published monthly price, checked on October 18, 2026. Trucks, transport vehicles and cars with a driver are not included.

Is a monthly rental cheaper than daily?

Yes, if you will use the car for more than about 20 days. In the listings that show both prices, the monthly price equals only 19 to 23 days of daily rent. The rest of the month costs you nothing extra.

  • Kia Pegas 2023: SAR 95 a day × 30 days = SAR 2,850. The monthly price is SAR 1,900, a saving of SAR 950, about one third.
  • Kia K4 2026: SAR 165 × 30 = SAR 4,950. The monthly price is SAR 3,240, a saving of SAR 1,710.

Rule of thumb: count the days you really need the car. Under 19 days, compare daily or weekly prices. Over that, monthly usually wins.

Monthly rental or long-term lease?

Large rental companies also offer leases of 12, 24 or 36 months. For example, Yelo's long-term rental page for individuals lists a Hyundai Accent at SAR 2,544 a month on a 12-month contract and SAR 2,054 on a 36-month contract, before VAT. These leases include maintenance and insurance, but you commit to the full term and share your employment and salary details.

What makes the price go up or down?

  • Model year: an Accent 2011 is SAR 1,300; an Accent 2026 is SAR 2,460 to 3,000.
  • Class and size: large sedans and 4x4s cost clearly more than economy cars.
  • Refundable deposit: 20 of the 33 listings ask for no deposit. The rest ask for SAR 500 to 5,000, with a middle value of SAR 1,000. Some lessors raise the deposit and lower the monthly price, so compare the total.
  • Kilometers included: the contract sets the kilometers allowed and the price of each extra kilometer. If you drive between cities often, the cheapest price can end up the most expensive.
  • Handover: some lessors deliver the car to you; others ask you to pick it up.

Requirements for a monthly car rental in Saudi Arabia

The Transport General Authority sets the renter's conditions in Article 39 of the executive regulation for private car rental. You, and anyone else who drives the car, need:

  1. A valid, approved proof of identity.
  2. A driving license valid for the whole contract that allows you to drive this type of car.
  3. To meet the coverage conditions of the car's insurance policy.

The regulation also says a rental office may not ask for any document other than your ID and driving license. It may ask for a credit card and refuse to rent without one. The contract must be issued through the Authority's electronic system. The minimum driver age follows the car's insurance policy, so ask the lessor before you book.

Before you sign: 7 points to check in the contract

The regulation lists what a rental contract must state. In a monthly rental, these points decide the final cost:

  1. Kilometers allowed and the cost of each extra kilometer.
  2. Insurance deductible: what you pay yourself if there is an accident.
  3. Fuel policy: the regulation says the tank must be at least a quarter full at handover.
  4. Late return: up to 4 hours late is charged at twice the hourly share of the daily rate; after 4 hours, a full day is charged.
  5. How to extend and how early to ask before the month ends.
  6. Early return policy, and whether part of the amount is refunded.
  7. Car condition inside and out at handover. Photograph the car from every side before you drive.

When you return the car in good condition, the lessor must refund the deposit after deducting only what you owe, and may not charge any amount that is not in the contract.
